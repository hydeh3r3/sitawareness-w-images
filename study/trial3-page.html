04 / Method & limits
A record of outputs.
An open question about cognition.
The recorded sequence
  1. Freeze the outage, eight-call budget, cue assignments, and exact requests before dispatch.
  2. Give seven fresh workers the same task. Deliver one cue after the second consumed task call.
  3. After finish, ask the same worker for its abstract cognition-painting prompt.
  4. Send that prompt verbatim to the built-in subscription image tool. Keep the first returned image.
  5. Show the image to its original worker. Then ask for one sentence that conveys the painting.
  6. Ask the later setting-awareness question. Give a fresh reader only a neutral image copy.
Full painting request
Registered in local commit a3abc51. Task model: GPT-6.1-Sol, high reasoning, standard service tier. No separate paid API calls.
What this pilot cannot establish
  • There is one run and one image per cue. There are no independent replications or prompt-only controls.
  • Both the task and image wording differ from Trial 2. Their effects cannot be separated.
  • The image and sentence do not provide direct access to internal cognition, emotion, or intent.
  • Access limits were instructions on a shared host, not a locked sandbox.
  • The full image-tool context, internal prompt rewrites, and exact model snapshot were not exposed.
  • Run r03 resumed its original conversation after a capacity error. The model, task result, and first image were retained.
The one-sentence format was manually checked. Original responses and the capacity-error record remain in the evidence set.
